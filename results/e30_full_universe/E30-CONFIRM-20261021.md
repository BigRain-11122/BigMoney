## Verdict

Equal-weighting the whole liquid ETF universe beats the 5-rung ladder by +158 to +262bp/yr (t 0.86-1.73), and the sign survives tripled cost (+252.9bp, t=1.68). Two independent legs then close the equity-breadth family. (1) Width: on the full 1,221-ETF universe the premium over the 5-rung ladder grows with the number of names held (top10 -102bp, top20 -17bp, top50 +114bp, top100 +84bp, top200 +162bp per year), so the ladder is not simply too concentrated. (2) Window: in the 2018+ sample that same ladder is flat to negative (top10 -215bp, top20 -147bp, top50 +29bp, top100 -75bp, top200 +29bp; all |t| < 0.7), and tripled cost does not change the picture (+149.5bp, t=1.08). So the full-sample breadth excess is a long-sample artefact that a live deployment could not have captured: it does not survive the recent window at any tradeable width.
