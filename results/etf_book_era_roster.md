## What this face will not say

- the roster is the ETF list that exists TODAY; names that listed before 2015 and died before 2026 are absent, so 2015's 61 readable names is an undercount and the breadth multiple is a floor in the other direction -- it compares surviving-2015 names with surviving-2026 names
- `full_roster` equal-weights EVERY readable name, so it is bound by the weakest corner of the roster, not by the book the company runs; read it as the market's width, not as a strategy
- `depth_k5` picks the five deepest names, which is a different book from the score book by construction -- it is the control that removes the ranking
- a name counts toward the roster only if it printed a bar within the last 5 sessions: a name that stopped trading keeps answering `adv20_at` with its old median, and counting it would put dead funds in today's roster
- `n_slots_at_budget` counts names that can absorb the gate's own per-slot order (lens/5) at the line -- a count, so one micro-fund cannot move it
- `names` in the five-slot split is measured on the survivors of the left-hand picks, PER SLOT: a pick that does not trade at the right-hand anchor is named and excluded rather than priced at zero, and the loss of that slot is not booked as a name effect -- the book-to-book multiple beside it still prices five slots at both ends
- the ADV reading is a 20-bar median as of each bar, so every year is measured with the liquidity it had
