## What this does not say

- no book is re-priced here: every yearly row is copied from a published receipt
- the partial year is not annualised; the hurdle is the return needed over the remaining cohorts, which is a different number from an annualised rate
- a finite distance is not a forecast: it says what the current year must deliver for the calendar gate to pass, not what it will deliver
- distance is measured on the return of one year; a book can also be re-cut, and that path is not priced here
