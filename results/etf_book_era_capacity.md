## Honest boundaries

- the picks on each year's first bar are ranked by the same score row the book uses today, applied to data up to that bar; they are not the picks the book actually held in that year (it launched in 2020)
- the ADV reading is a 20-bar median as of that bar, so a year is measured with the liquidity it had, not with today's
- the universe is the ETF list that exists TODAY -- names that delisted before 2026 are absent, which is the book's standing survivorship bias and is not fixed by this face
- a year whose five slots cannot all be measured prints no reading rather than a smaller one (the gate's line is written for five equal slots)
- capacity in CNY is the largest capital that fills the WEAKEST of five equal slots at the line; a book that sizes unevenly would carry a different number
