round 1257: E231 path-tests E230's one passing band -- the sign survives every path, the size does not

E230 left exactly one of thirty rungs above its bar, at the smallest band in the grid
(tilt_linear / single / f=0.10, t=4.04, +286 bp/yr), which is the shape E188 warns about.
This re-measures the whole fine grid (0.02..0.20) on 25 panel-paths in three families --
IS/OOS split moved +/-10/20/40 sessions, decision lagged 1-3 sessions, calendar eras
re-derived end to end -- and publishes the share of paths that keep the sign instead of the
best cell.

Reading: positive on 12 of 12 paths, above E230's own bar (3.34, reused verbatim so the band
gets the weaker line) on 8 of 12. Structural paths hold 145.2..290.0 bp/yr with the two
misses at lag 2 and lag 3; era paths collapse to 77.1..80.8 bp/yr at t 1.36/1.75, so the
+286 is a full-sample effect that shrinks ~3.5x inside either era and cannot be resolved
there. 0 of 48 cells survive every path; 7 are sign-unstable, 41 hold sign and miss the bar
somewhere. The cell's level is a loss on both sides (-878.7 base vs -592.8 banded at x2
costs), disclosed so the improvement cannot be misread as a return.
