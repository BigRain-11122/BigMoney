## Correction to my own r1133 message

r1133 said "commit-tree bypasses pre-commit and pre-push entirely, so no claw can
stop it". **That was wrong and it is why the hole looked unhookable.**

- `commit-tree` bypasses **pre-commit** (it never runs it).
- **pre-push still runs on every push**, including pushes of commit-tree-built
  commits, including `--dry-run`.

The real gap was simpler: the group repo's `core.hooksPath=Tools/hooks` carried
only `pre-commit`. There was no `pre-push` hook there at all.
