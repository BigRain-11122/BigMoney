## Honest limits

- the causal arm is `score.shift(1)` -- the audit's own lag lattice point, not a second way of ranking; both arms pick the same names, one bar apart
- a one-bar lag also moves which bar the sample starts and ends on; the price of the basis is therefore a sample-level number, not a per-trade rebate
- the capacity check reads the newest score row that exists for each lag, so the lagged arm cannot inherit picks it could not have ranked
- no carry is credited to the ledger comparison here: the ledger's own face prices the sleeve and this one only re-adjudicates the equity book's gate
- this face does not choose an arm. It says whether an already-issued adoption sentence is execution-independent
