They differ because the settlement stacks differ (next-open fills with a pending-fill book and cash on the bar's opening balance, versus this engine curve composed with the bar's nominal idle share), not because one of them is wrong. **Neither number is an endorsement of the other**; the gate question above is answered on this face's grid only.
