## Honest limits

- an exchange repo contract cannot be broken early; the `break` arm is the cost of an emergency funding line standing in for that impossibility, and its number is a lower bound on the price of being wrong about the calendar
- the ledger keeps balances in units of NAV, matching the engine's own convention that weights do not drift between rebalances (the same convention the published idle-cash face uses); a drifting sleeve would move these numbers by less than the disclosed rounding
- `callable` uses the longest ladder tenor that matures before the NEXT SCHEDULED decision bar -- knowing your own rebalance date is not a forecast, but it does assume the treasurer keeps that calendar, and it assumes a rung can be placed at that bar's close
- the sleeve is re-placed on the book's own bar calendar, so a maturity that lands on a non-bar day waits for the next bar; the interest is booked to the bar the cash actually returns, not the nominal maturity date
- `2026` is a partial calendar year and is flagged partial by the gate's own 60-bar rule, exactly as in the published cells
- the book is the gate's 5-name arm; the forward ledger runs 12 names with the same carrier, and r1739 already showed width does not change the carrier verdict, so this face does not re-run width
