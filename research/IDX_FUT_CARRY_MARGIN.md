**判词：VOL_RULER_IS_THE_GATE**

- 保证金不是门槛：保证金臂要追平波动臂首档，需要初始保证金率到 90.7%、97.5%（IC/IM 各自的临界值），而现实中券商收 12-15%。所以开仓资金线仍由 8% 波动尺决定；保证金只吃掉其中一部分现金，不改变 1 手首档。反过来说——若改用保证金口径，账户能开的杠杆是波动预算的若干倍，风险会失控，这正是不能拿保证金当尺子的原因。

## 这份面不改什么

- the desk's lots (an input, printed and marked elsewhere, never rewritten)
- the 8% vol target (road A stands; this face only asks whether margin would have overruled it, and at 10/12/14/15% it does not)
- the forward paper series (kept as evidence, never truncated)

## 什么是测出来的、什么是规则参数

测出来的：
- notional per lot at the panel's own close (multiplier cross-checked against the notional basis the capital face already published)
- worst adverse move actually printed, roll days excluded
- breakeven margin rate = vol ruler first rung / notional (pure arithmetic on two published numbers)

规则参数（扫描，不作为本书的测度）：
- the initial-margin percentage: exchange rule plus broker add-on; swept, never presented as a measurement of this book
