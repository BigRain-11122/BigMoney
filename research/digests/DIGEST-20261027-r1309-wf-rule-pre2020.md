# Does the RULE survive, or only the hindsight cell?

r1309 bm-c, 2026-10-27. Artifacts: `results/etf_book_pre2020_wf.json` (schema
`etf_book_pre2020_wf/v1`). Code: `scripts/etf_book_pre2020_wf.py` (selftest 8/8, one new
smoke leg). Method card: E145.

## The gap r1308 left

r1308 closed the two-readings problem with a four-rung ladder and asserted both ends to
0.0pp. Every rung was a *fixed* cell: bond-inclusive N5, bond-free N5, N12, N12+overlay.
But the company does not deploy a fixed cell -- the shipped rule re-picks `(N, R)` every
year on trailing Sharpe (`etf_walkforward_capacity.py`, r1256). A ladder of fixed cells
cannot say whether that rule survives the window in which the published cell prints
-4.97%/yr.

## What was priced

One universe (the 193 production-panel names whose first bar is <= 2020-01-01, bond/money
funds already excluded, 168 quote-face flips repaired), one window (2015-01-01 ..
2019-12-31, lower bound passed to the engine so the 120-bar history counter is not reset),
one execution rule (decision at close t, fill at open t+1, 10bp/side on the weight turned
over), one sizing rule (8% de-lever-only overlay on both arms). Selection is made on the
raw curve -- the shipped rule -- and the year is earned on the overlay curve of the picked
cell, because the overlay sizes whatever book you hold; it is not a property of the cell.

## Answer: the rule lands where the fixed cell lands, for a reason that is not skill

| arm | 2016-2019 chained | yearly |
|---|---|---|
| walk-forward rule | **4.86%** | 20.91% cumulative |
| fixed N12 + overlay | 4.97% | 21.42% |
| equal weight, same universe | 2.69% | 11.20% |

Yearly picks and the trailing Sharpe the selector saw at decision time:

| year | picked | trailing SR | that year |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2016 | N8_R40 | 0.243 | -2.69% |
| 2017 | N8_R40 | -0.077 | +13.04% |
| 2018 | N8_R40 | 0.136 | -11.29% |
| 2019 | N3_R40 | -0.034 | +23.90% |

All nine overlay cells are positive on this window: 3.80% .. 5.98%, mean **5.14%**,
sd **0.79pp**, 9/9 positive. So the walk-forward arm is within 0.28pp of the *mean of the
nine cells* and within 0.11pp of the production width: the selector had almost nothing to
choose between (its own trailing Sharpe readings hovered around zero), and the four picks
were effectively a draw. "The rule survives 2015-2019" is arithmetic -- nine positive
cells under the overlay -- and must not be read as selection skill.

Control: both fixed arms re-price the published ladder through this script's own call path
at **0.00pp** (r3 N12 raw 0.73% published / 0.73% recomputed; r4 N12+overlay 5.12% / 5.12%,
tolerance 0.5pp, asserted in the artifact). Without that, the three deltas above would be a
code difference rather than a selection result.

## What it changes and what it does not

- It does not promote the pre-2020 premium to evidence. 193 names, four trading years, a
  survival-filtered universe (no delisted funds in the panel), and a selector that had no
  dispersion to exploit.
- It does retire the reading that the book "does not work before 2020". The -4.97% belongs
  to the bond-inclusive, 5-name, unsized cell (r1308 ladder rung 1), which is not the
  deployed configuration; the deployed rule earns 4.86%/yr there and the deployment width
  earns 4.97%, both against 2.69% equal weight.
- It adds one mechanical law: chained yearly returns must share their boundary bar
  (first bar >= Jan 1 of Y and of Y+1, both included), otherwise the chain drops the move
  between the last December bar and the first January bar and stops equalling the same
  curve's window return. Selftest L5 asserts chain == curve return on a constant-pick
  fixture; L4 asserts the chain equals the product of the years it publishes.
