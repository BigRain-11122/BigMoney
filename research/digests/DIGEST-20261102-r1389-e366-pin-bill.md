# DIGEST r1389 -- E366: the queue is thirteen studies and a bill of two pins

Round: r1389 (bm-b) · study `e366_pinable_queue_size` · face `results/state_tilt/PINQUEUE-2026-11-02.json`

## What was asked

r1387's carry-over (pool card `E366-WHICH-FACE-POPULATION-STUDIES-CAN-BE-PINNED-TODAY`): E364
counted 23 studies that enumerate the published faces and 15 that quote a population; E365 proved
the pin-and-replay mechanism works. Measure, per study, whether it has the `files=` seam, whether it
has a store-able minority, and what its pin count would be -- *so the queue becomes work-sized
instead of one study per round*.

## What was measured

Four measurements, each from the studies' own code rather than a re-implementation:

| reading | value |
|---|---|
| queued studies (E364's own queue, own face excluded) | 13 |
| distinct populations the queue walks | **2** |
| pins required | **2** — 7 studies over published faces, 6 over the source corpus |
| six `corpus()` populations: union / intersection | 2,377 / 2,371, every delta = that study's own file |
| source population (widest walk, `e346._corpus()`) | 2,387 members, 112 need a store (0.52 MB) |
| face population (`e328.scan_faces()`) | 593 members, 65 need a store (3.0 MB) |
| seam shapes over the 13 studies | 2 `build(files=)` · 1 local shadow · 6 prose-only · 4 absent |
| studies that reach *both* populations | 5 |

The two families are nested, not merely similar: `e323.corpus()` ⊂ `e346._corpus()` with
`only_e346 = 11` (all `content/drama/runner_assets/*.py`), `only_e323 = 0`.

## Why the unit moved three times

1. **Studies → populations.** Importing all six `corpus()` functions gives 2,372..2,375 members.
   Equal counts would have proved nothing; the *sets* did, and every difference is a study
   excluding itself (E319). Counting rows would have billed thirteen pins for two walks.
2. **Members → store minority.** 109 of the 2,387 source members are untracked scratch files the
   repo forbids committing (E361's finding, here priced: 0.52 MB), and 66 of 593 faces (3.0 MB).
   Git already holds the rest, so the store, not the hash, is the expensive half.
3. **Seams → parameters.** E364 published its own limit ("a build() that takes files and ignores it
   would still read as a seam"). Measured, the word `files` appears in six build bodies that have
   no parameter -- format strings, and `files, skipped = [], []`, which is a list of *records* and a
   different population entirely. A text grep calls nine seams; two are real.

## Verdict

`the_queued_studies_walk_two_populations_so_the_bill_is_two_pins_not_thirteen`

Two studies are pinnable today (E328 and E329 already take `build(files=)`), ten need a seam, and one
(E340, quoting 12,519 -- far beyond the 593 faces on disk) needs its own rule before any pin applies.

## The pit this round

The first build reported a 4,764-member source population. Two walkers return the same files in
different address forms -- `e346._corpus()` absolute, the `corpus()` studies repo-relative -- so the
union double-counted every member. Equal *counts* across studies were the signal that made it
findable; a population is only comparable once its members are normalised to one path form.
