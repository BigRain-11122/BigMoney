## 一、深捕获双标的（摘要逐字忠实转述）

### 1.1 【标的一】The Illusory Nature of Momentum Profits（Lesmond-Schill-Zhou · JFE 71(2) 2004-02 · pp.349-380）

一手指针：DOI `10.1016/s0304-405x(03)00206-x`（被引 508）；SSRN preprint 2003 DOI `10.2139/ssrn.256926`。CrossRef 摘要 verbatim：

> In markets with trading friction, the incorporation of information into market prices can be substantially delayed through a weakening of the arbitrage process. We re-examine the profitability of relative-strength, or momentum, trading strategies (buying past strong performers and selling past weak performers). We find that standard relative-strength strategies require frequent trading in disproportionately high-cost securities so that trading costs prevent profitable strategy execution. In the cross section, we find that those stocks that generate large momentum returns are precisely those stocks with high trading costs. We conclude that the magnitude of the abnormal returns associated with these trading strategies creates an illusion of profit opportunity when, in fact, none exists.

三机制要点（拆解）：①摩擦市场套利弱化→信息入价延迟＝动量可预测性**存在**的前提；②标准动量需在高成本证券上高频换手→成本吃掉全部可执行利润；③**横截面集中律**：大动量收益股恰=高交易成本股——异常幅度与执行成本正相耦合。

参考文献深挖指针（CrossRef 全录内）：Korajczyk-Sadka《Are momentum profits robust to trading costs?》（ssrn.305282）+ Lesmond et al. 1999《A new estimate of transaction costs》(RFS 12.5.1113)＝成本估计方法族两指针，仅登记。

### 1.2 【标的二】Is Trend Still Your Friend? A Microstructural Account of the Demise of Short-Term Trend-Following（Kurth-Eisler-Rej-Bouchaud · arXiv 2607.01550 · 2026-07-02）

一手指针：`arxiv.org/abs/2607.01550`（免费全文 PDF/HTML）；q-fin.TR/q-fin.PM；20 页 21 图 11 页附录。arXiv Atom 摘要 verbatim（节选关键句）：

> Systematic trend following has, on average, been profitable for at least two centuries; yet since approximately 2009, short-term trends have ceased to deliver reliable returns. … We evaluate four candidate explanations – capacity constraints, market electronification, a regime change in CTA-versus-order-flow interactions, and a microstructural mechanism – and find that the first three fail on grounds of timing, magnitude, or cross-sectional heterogeneity. Our central empirical finding is that the cross-sectional variable distinguishing degraded from surviving trends is the volatility-normalised tick size: post-2008 trend PnL has collapsed on small-tick contracts across all signal horizons, while remaining essentially intact on large-tick ones. … a self-fulfilling feedback loop … trend signals trigger directional trades, whose market impact reinforces the very price moves that generated the signal. … the post-crisis transition to HFT-dominated market making … has broken this loop on small-tick contracts.

**跨源校验**（slice-2 Swedroe 评述 vs 本片一手摘要）：三核心声称（速度依赖断裂/波动率归一化 tick size 切割/HFT 做市反馈环断裂机制）与四候选解释证伪（容量/电子化/CTA-订单流/微观结构）**逐条吻合**＝评述忠实度 PASS；评述独有面（LeBaron 效应双佐证、Martone COI 保留意见）在论文正文不在摘要——正文深挖留待未来片（免费全文在案）。
