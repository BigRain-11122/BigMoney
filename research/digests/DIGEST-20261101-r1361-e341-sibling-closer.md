### E341 the census sibling closer is two commits in one body -- and only one half is visible in the name currency

E338 said the 15/15 windows close on the next sibling and that its two readings coincided. This prices the closer instead of naming it. Each closer adds a census study **and** edits `Tools/s6_chain` in the same commit (18 census studies, 16 wiring edits, 15 distinct commits).

1. **Nothing outside the family ever closes these windows, so "the corpus moved under the face" is false.** The corpus moves *because* the sibling arrived. Any face that treats corpus drift as an external nuisance is measuring its own publication event.
2. **The wiring half is invisible in the name currency and visible in every counting currency.** The added study names its own face in 16/16 windows (a stem with 0 pre-existing writers) and names an already-published stem in 8/16; the wiring edit moves **0 names** while adding **360 lines / 23,863 bytes** in place. Faces that count sources/lines move; faces that resolve "who names this face" do not. That is the mechanical reason E338's two readings coincided -- both were name-currency readings over a commit whose visible half is the added file.
3. **The price of self-exclusion is a number, not an opinion.** At the pinned tree, dropping the 16 family files costs 0.72% of sources and 1.31% of lines, removes 29 writer rows, and leaves **15 of 22** measured names with no writer at all -- names published by nobody except the census family. So excluding the family is a population definition with a measured cost: it deletes the family's self-measurements, which are most of what the family measures.
4. **A name must be published under `results/`, or selftest fixtures enter the census.** Unfiltered, stems came from the studies' own fake arms (`NAME-%s.json`, `OUT-...`, `AAA-...`, `QUIET`): 34 stems of which 12 were fixtures, and the exclusion table listed `AAA`/`FOO`/`QUIET` as orphaned names. Gate: count a stem only if some face under `results/` is published under it. 34 -> 22.
5. **`x or -1` is not a missing-value guard when 0 is legal.** The first smoke leg asserted `writers - rows_removed == writers_without_the_family` and fired on a row whose `writers_without_the_family` is legitimately 0, because `int(v or -1)` turns 0 into -1. A count field's floor is not its sentinel -- check `is None`, not truthiness.
