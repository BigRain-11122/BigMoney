## S1 — B6 风险平价 + All Weather【过闸·全验】

源：EN Wikipedia "Risk parity"（fetched 2026-09-28，oldid=1360571435；二次源 Callan 2010/WSJ 2013-06-27/FT 2012/GMO Inker 2010 皆经该文转引）。

- 起源（逐字）："the first risk parity fund, called the All Weather fund, was pioneered in 1996"（Bridgewater）；"The term, risk parity, came into use in 2005, coined by Edward Qian, of PanAgora"。
- 机制核：等风险贡献（ERC）：σ_i(w)=σ(w)/N，协方差凸优化有唯一解；"focuses on allocation of risk, usually defined as volatility, rather than allocation of capital"；Bob Prince（桥水 CIO）定三参数："uncorrelated assets, low equity risk, and passive management"。
- 失效史（判据相关的诚实锚）：
  - 逐字："Risk parity is vulnerable to significant shifts in correlation regimes, such as observed in Q1 2020, which led to the significant underperformance of risk-parity funds in the COVID-19 sell-off."（相关性政体突变=失效模式）
  - Callan 2010：90 年代杠杆化 RP 组合"would have significantly underperformed"，2000-2010 十年"would have significantly outperformed"——**政体依赖实证**（非全天候）。
  - WSJ 2013-06-27 标题面："Fashionable 'Risk Parity' Funds Hit Hard"。
  - Inker 批评：RP 需过度杠杆以匹配常规组合期望收益；债券负偏度（小概率大损）不宜杠杆。
  - 有序/无序加息政体区分（BlackRock 文献面）："Risk parity has weaker performance in disorderly rising rates environments but its performance over time is not dependent on falling bond yields."
- 映射我环：≈环⑤组合配置（B_MAXDIV 近亲：波动率倒数权重=ERC 一阶近似）。机制差：多：波动率倒数/ERC 数学化+杠杆工程；少：无题材/情绪面、A 股 T+1 工具域杠杆受限（ETF 两融门槛）。
- 可迁移件：**波动率目标映射=仓位阶梯的方差版**（已在册 B6 行）；失效模式「相关性政体突变」=我方政体四态的旁证（股债双杀政体需独立态）。
- 拥挤标注：机构标配（2014 调查 46% 机构在用）=**高拥挤**；其 alpha 端已折入 beta 化。
