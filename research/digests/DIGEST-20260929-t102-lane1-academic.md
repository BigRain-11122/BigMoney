## S1 — B3 Markov 机制切换模型族【过闸·全验】

源：Hamilton, J.D. (1989) "A New Approach to the Economic Analysis of Nonstationary Time Series and the Business Cycle", *Econometrica* 57(2), 357-384（JSTOR stable/1912559 摘要面 + UCSD Hamilton 自存 Palgrave 综述 PDF econweb.ucsd.edu/~jhamilto/palgrav1.pdf；检索窗 2026-09-29）。

- 起源（逐字，JSTOR 摘要）："arise if the process is subject to discrete shifts in regime—episodes across which the dynamic behavior of the series is markedly different"；"My basic approach is to use Goldfeld and Quandt's (1973) Markov-switching regression"（检索快照渲染 'Quand's'=显示伪影，正确拼法 Quandt）。
- 判据面（判据相关的诚实锚，逐字）："the periodic shift from a positive growth rate to a negative growth rate is a recurrent feature of the U.S. business cycle, and indeed could be used as an objective criterion for defining and measuring economic recessions"——**政体判定的学术正典做法=目标序列自身的增长符号切换作客观判据**（替代 NBER 主观委员会判定）。
- 机制核：两态隐状态（扩张/衰退）+转移概率矩阵；观测方程=状态条件 AR；Hamilton 滤波→平滑状态概率=**概率化政体判定**。族谱活跃度旁证：REStat 98(5)（MIT Press 直达）"Markov-Switching Models with Evolving Regime-specific Parameters"放宽恒定状态均值=族仍在正典演化中。
- 映射我环：≈环①温度计（B3 行 v0.1 判断成立）。机制差：多=概率化隐状态+MLE 估计；少=可解释性/滞后性/**状态载体选择问题**——Hamilton 正典用政策目标序列自身（GNP）定态，我方 v3 用 510300+core48 面板=**同构成立**（无需改）；概率化输出=v1.3 提案候选维持（§三 v0.1 判断）。
- 拥挤标注：学术正典（引用数巨大）；A 股移植=券商金工普遍做过（时序不稳定风险）。

## S2 — B4a 动量腿：时序动量 MOP 2012【过闸·全验】

源：Moskowitz, Ooi, Pedersen "Time Series Momentum", *JFE* 2012, 104(2), 228-250（ScienceDirect S0304405X11002613 + SSRN 2089463 + NYU Stern 自存 PDF）。

- 判据面（逐字）："12-month time series momentum profits are positive, not just on average across these assets, but for every asset contract we examine (58 in total)"；"related to, but different from, the phenomenon known as 'momentum' in the finance literature, which is primarily cross-sectional in nature"（ScienceDirect 摘要）；"persistence in returns for one to 12 months that partially reverses over longer horizons"（CBS 记录）；"performs best during extreme markets"（SSRN 摘要）。
- 机制核：过去 12 个月自身收益符号→多空开关；58 品种=股指/外汇/商品/债期四类。
- 诚实锚：1-12 月持续**长期部分反转**=时序动量的政体切换风险面；"extreme markets 表现最优"=**政体条件化的学术旁证**（我方 GREEN×HOT 加帽面的外部证据）。
- 映射我环：≈环②偏好面（T-101 H1 风格倾斜同向）；我方时序门族（RSV60/30 等）=同族不同支。机制差：多=跨资产正典证据；少=A 股 T+1/单市场 ETF 域适配。
- 拥挤标注：因子圈正典·机构实施面已折价。

## S3 — B4b 套息腿：Carry KMPV【过闸·全验】

源：Koijen, Moskowitz, Pedersen, Vrugt "Carry"，NBER w19325（2013）/SSRN 2298565 工作稿→2018 刊出版（ScienceDirect PII S0304405X17302908=JFE 卷宗；VU 记录 2018/2——**venue 双记录如实注记，刊卷页码以 PII 卷宗为准**）。

- 判据面（逐字，NBER 摘要）："A security's expected return can be decomposed into its 'carry' and its expected price appreciation, where carry can be measured in advance without an asset pricing model"；"carry predicts returns both in the cross section and time series for a variety of different asset classes"（含 global equities/bonds/currencies/commodities/US Treasuries/credit/options）；（ScienceDirect 摘要）"cannot be explained by other known global return factors such as value, momentum, and time series momentum"。
- 机制核：carry=事前可测的持有收益（零模型）；跨资产横截面+时序双预测力。
- 映射我环：≈环②偏好面输入候选。**A 股 ETF 域 carry 载体窄**（无期货 carry 主载体）→原生载体=国债逆回购期限梯利率（T-88 采集面在仓）+货基/QDII 腿。**仓内先例**：INNOVATION-QUOTA REPO-CALENDAR 族已判 GC014/GC028 过 G1 线（DSR 1.0）=期限梯 carry 机制在仓内独立验证链已有正锚——本 S3=该族的外部正典旁证。
- 可迁移：逆回购利率带结构（季末/节前钱荒尖峰=GC001 2015-02-10 53.44 实证）=**流动性政体信号候选**（与 B8 情绪三轴正交：资金面×情绪面双轴）。
- 拥挤标注：学术正典+机构实施面。

## S4 — B4c 防御腿：BAB 低 beta 异象【过闸·全验】

源：Frazzini, Pedersen "Betting Against Beta", *JFE* 2014, 111(1), 1-25（ScienceDirect S0304405X13002675 + docs.lhpedersen.com 自存 PDF + CBS 记录）。

- 判据面（逐字）："self-financing portfolio that is long the low-beta portfolio and short the high-beta portfolio"；（CBS 摘要）模型="leverage and margin constraints that vary across investors and time"；五中心预测之一："Because constrained investors bid up high-beta assets, high beta is associated with low alpha"。
- 机制核：杠杆约束投资者抬高高 beta 资产→低 beta 长期超额（约束套利）。
- 映射我环：≈环②偏好面防御腿+环④仓位阶梯（RED/ORANGE 态防御倾斜）。A 股映射=红利低波 ETF 族（CEO 导向清单在册族）。与 CEO 风险偏好律（高收益高回撤族入池）非互斥=**攻守两腿的配置问题非关线判据**。
- 可迁移：RED/ORANGE 态组合内防御倾斜判据组（低波/红利面）=T-101 v4 政体门候选臂输入。
- 拥挤标注：学术正典+smart beta 产品化拥挤。
