## honesty notes

* no volatility, price or P&L is derived here: capital, per-lot vol, admissible lots, first rung and the desk's lots are read from the sizing spec, which reads the ladder, which reads the paper SIZING face;
* `--check` re-derives the funding line, the gap, the executable lots and the labels from those inputs, and names what it did not recompute;
* 'cheapest to open' compares accounts on their own first rung -- it is not a preference between the two hedges (that is priced in the hedge face);
* the withdrawal is conditional on capital, not a verdict on the sleeve: the same rule hands back one lot at the first rung;
* the paper ledger is opened read-only by this file and is never written.
