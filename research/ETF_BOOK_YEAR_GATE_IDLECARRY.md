## Boundary

- the cash vehicle is a daily GC001 roll at the published rate, not a dealer quote; one contract per bar, exchange commission included, and its 1.77%/yr is reproduced here rather than assumed
- the composed curve is a return statement about the book, not a ticket: it says what the sleeve would have earned had it been paid, not that the desk earned it
- idle is 1 - scale held constant over a holding period (the engine's own nominal weight convention); a drifting sleeve moves the number below the printed precision

- instrument-list survivorship is unchanged and still disclosed on the card; this face does not repair it
